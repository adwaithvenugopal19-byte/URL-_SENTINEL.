01 / The problem

Phishing links are designed to look trustworthy.

The challenge is not only spotting a bad link. It is helping people make a safer decision before sharing credentials or payment details.

◈

Deceptive identity

Brand-like words, lookalike domains and unusual structures can make malicious links appear legitimate.

⌘

Technical warning signs

HTTP, raw IP addresses, punycode, unusual ports, long paths and encoded characters may indicate risk.

◎

The human decision gap

People need actionable explanations and safer next steps — not just a black-box safe or unsafe verdict.

02 / The solution

From link to evidence to decision.

URLSentinel inspects URL text, scores measurable indicators, and explains the evidence behind its assessment.

STEP 03

STEP 04

Classify

0–29 low, 30–59 suspicious, 60+ high risk.

STEP 05

Explain

Show evidence and recommend a safer action.

04 / Methodology

How the score is built.

A rule-based engine adds points for observable indicators. The final score is capped at 100, and each signal is shown for transparency.

CategoryIndicatorPoints
DomainRaw IP address used as host+25
DomainPunycode / internationalized domain marker+20
ProtocolHTTP (no transport encryption)+20
Structure@ symbol in URL+15
TermsAuthentication / brand-like keywords+8 each, max 30
StructureUnusual port, long URL or many subdomains+10 each
DomainHyphenated host or higher-abuse TLD heuristic+8 each
EncodingEncoded characters or many parameters+10

The browser demo uses illustrative heuristics, not a trained model or live threat-intelligence feed. Scores are indicators, not proof of malicious activity.

05 / Scope & roadmap

Honest scope. Clear next steps.

The goal is to make safer decisions easier while being transparent about what a URL-only scan can and cannot determine.

◷

Current limitations

  • Inspects URL text, not the destination page content.
  • Uses hand-set rules rather than a trained classifier.
  • Cannot confirm a site is safe from a low score alone.
↗

Future enhancements

  • Machine-learning classifier trained on labelled phishing data.
  • Threat-intelligence and domain-age lookups.
  • Optional scan history and analytics dashboard.
  • Browser extension for in-context warnings.
